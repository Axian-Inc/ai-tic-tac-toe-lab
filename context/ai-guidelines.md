# AI Agent Guidelines

## Project-Specific Rules
- Keep the solution dependency-free unless explicitly approved.
- Favor clarity and simplicity over advanced patterns for this POC.

## Preferences
- **Code style**: Small modules with explicit state and pure functions.
- **Communication**: Provide concise steps and confirm decisions before expanding scope.

## Context Update Triggers
- Update context files when UX behavior, AI styles, or deployment details change.
