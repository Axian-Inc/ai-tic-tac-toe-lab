# Prompt
Lets update the coding-standards to specify how a new story should be processed.  When beginning a new story, the story should be reviewed to esure that it is granualr enough and should not be decomposed into smaller stories, the story should be reviewed for completeness and any clarfications should be made before continueing.  Once a story is ready to begin, then a test-first plan should be created and the planning steps appended to the story for refernce.  As each step is completed it will be verified and updated in the story.  Any additional development or testing notes will be appended to the story.
